We’re here again, in all our power, many thousands strong,
As we stand in solidarity and raise an urgent song!
We’ve come together, black and white, and tan and red and brown,
To say that we’re not gonna’ let these fascists beat us down.

Oh, we’ve fought and died for voting rights; a right we must not lose;
And we stand with “me too” women takin’ down those who abuse.
We want Medicare for All (the Bill called H six sev-en six,)
And public schools need funding, not just a one-time fix!

CHORUS #1

People power! Together we will stand
To reclaim our rights with all our might
Across this troubled land!

We’ve the right to love who e’er we choose, the right to have a home,
The right to safe streets free from guns wherever we might roam,
The right of folks from other lands to get full welcome here,
The right of Dreamers to fulfill the dreams that they hold dear.
We demand an end to wars and any torture in our name.
And our Social Safety Nets we will fight fiercely to retain.
No more drillin’. No more pipelines! Let’s go foss-il-free today.
Let’s empty all those prisons and throw all the keys away!
