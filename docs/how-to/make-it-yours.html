Making Your Gaggle's Site Your Own

Your front-page photo, your tagline, your extras: Gaggle Settings and Gaggle Notes

Every gaggle site starts with the same sensible setup. This guide covers the handful of choices that make yours feel like yours. All of it lives in one place, none of it is required, and every choice can be changed or undone any time.

1. Opening Gaggle Settings

You need: to be logged in to your site (see the Keeping Your Gaggle's Site Fresh guide).
  1. In the left-side menu, click Appearance, then Gaggle Settings. One page with five settings appears. That's the whole control panel.
  2. After any change below, click the blue Save Settings button at the bottom. Nothing takes effect until you save, so you can explore freely.
Screenshot to add: Appearance → Gaggle Settings in the left menu, and the settings page itself.

2. Your front-page photo

The big photo on your front page. Choose one of your gaggle in action: hats, banners, mid-song. A phone photo is fine if it's sharp.

  1. Next to Hero Image, click Choose image.
  2. Click Upload files and pick the photo from your computer (or choose one already in your library).
  3. Click the blue button to confirm, then Save Settings. Open your site's front page to admire it.
Want it gone? The Clear button next to the image puts back the standard photo.

3. Your tagline

The italic line under your gaggle's name on the front page. Until you write one, every site shows the shared line: "Older women in flowered hats, singing truth to power and joy to the streets. We do not retire quietly."

  1. Type your own line into the Tagline box. One or two sentences. Your rallying cry, your humor, your gaggle.
  2. Click Save Settings.

4. Your YouTube channel

If your gaggle has a YouTube channel, paste its address into the YouTube Channel URL box and save. A Videos page appears in your site's menu, filled from your channel automatically. No channel? Leave the box empty and no Videos page shows: nothing looks missing.

5. The two checkboxes

  • Display songs on this subsite: tick this and your site gets its own /songs/ page showing just your gaggle's songs from the shared library. Leave it unticked and the Songs button points to the full international library instead. Either way your songs stay in the big library.
  • Hide photos on Action lists: if most of your Actions have no photos, tick this for a cleaner, text-only look on your front page and Actions list. Photos on individual Action pages still show.

Tick or untick, then Save Settings.

6. Gaggle Notes: a home for the evergreen stuff

Actions are news; they scroll away as new ones arrive. Gaggle Notes are for things that should stay put: your songbook, a resource list, your gaggle's history, standing meeting details. They get their own spot near the top of your front page and never get buried.

  1. In the left-side menu, click Actions, then Add New (yes, the same place news goes: the next step is what makes it a Note).
  2. Write your title and content as usual.
  3. In the right-hand column, find Categories and tick Gaggle Notes.
  4. Click Publish. It appears on your front page under "From this gaggle," and stays out of the Recent Actions news feed.
Screenshot to add: the Categories box with Gaggle Notes ticked.
Six-plus notes? The front page lists the six newest and adds an "All Gaggle Notes" link to the rest automatically.
Change your mind about any of it, any time. Settings save over each other harmlessly, and notes can be edited or unpublished like any post. Questions to webgranny@gmail.com, always answered.
